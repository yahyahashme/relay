How the pool works

Money stays inside the contract. No one at Relay can move it.

Lenders hold shares and can withdraw unlent money at any time.

Example: A puts in $6,000 and B $4,000. A $100 sale uses $60 and $40; the $0.50 splits $0.30 and $0.20.

Protected by the buffer, never "guaranteed".

Sources: docs/context.md §5 (lender order and pool rules, Nov 16 2026) · Relay contract (approve_lender, deposit, withdraw, shares) · Relay maths, docs/product/assumptions.md · Central bank, loans under one year, via Diario El Mundo, 2 Sep 2026

Use for: who are your lenders, why would anyone lend, can lenders pick shops, can they get money out, is this a bank. Lenders don't pick shops: one pool, like an index fund. Every lender is approved in the contract after a business check and can be revoked, but can always withdraw unlent money.
