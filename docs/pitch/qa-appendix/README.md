# Q&A appendix (10 pages)

Backup pages for judges' questions on money and fraud, made by Rohan on Nov 16. One page per kind of question, each readable on its own, with sources at the bottom and the page number top right.

| Page | Topic | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The problem | Why this matters, who is hurt |
| 2 | Market and access | Market size, why El Salvador, how shops get digital dollars (Risk #1) |
| 3 | How a delivery gets paid | The four ways to pay, who carries a default, the fee each pays |
| 4 | Where the fee goes | The $1.50 split, how the fee slides with risk |
| 5 | Is the price fair? | "Is it predatory?", cards and cash costs, the legal maximum |
| 6 | How a shop earns credit | The 2-payment unlock, tiered growth, late penalties, default |
| 7 | When a shop doesn't pay | Loss layers (vouching is full recourse), buffer target, benchmarks |
| 8 | Who funds the credit | Lender order, lender returns, how the pool works |
| 9 | Unit economics | Revenue, costs, break-even ≈ 19 distributors, sensitivity, pilot year |
| 10 | Fraud and trust | The fraud-flag process, threats and what stops them (live vs planned) |

## Files
- `Relay-QA-Appendix.pdf`: print this. Pages are 16:9 (1920 × 1080 px), so print landscape and choose "Fit to page".
- `appendix.html`: the editable source, one `<section>` per page. Change the text, open it in Chrome, then Print → Save as PDF (margins: none, background graphics on). Each page's `<aside>` holds speaker notes on what not to claim; they don't print.

Numbers match `main` as of Nov 16 (contract `CBXUYASL…GP4J`, credit v3, `docs/product/assumptions.md`). If a number changes, update the matching page.
